Our Story

From a Single Brush to a Studio of 40 Artisans

In 2016, master painter Roy Ellison painted his neighbour’s nursery in exchange for a home-cooked lasagna. The walls were so flawless the street started booking him. A decade later, RoyPainter is a 40-person studio of licensed artisans, colour stylists and project managers.

We haven’t lost that first-house care. Every job — a single accent wall or a 400-unit portfolio — gets the same obsession: surgical cut-ins, zero dust, and a finish that survives real life.

Privacy Policy

Your trust is as important to us as a flawless finish. Here’s exactly how we handle your information.

Last UpdatedAugust 14, 2026 Applies ToAll website visitors & clients Contactprivacy@roypainter.com

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms, newsletter, account sign-up, and project communications. This includes your name, email address, phone number, property address, and project details. We also collect limited technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and device information to keep the site fast and secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to quote requests, bookings and support enquiries
  • To schedule consultations and coordinate project logistics
  • To send the décor newsletter you explicitly subscribe to
  • To improve our website, services and client experience
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage solely to remember your preferences — such as your theme (dark/light) and layout direction. We do not sell or share this data. You can clear local storage anytime in your browser settings without losing access to any feature.

4. Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell your personal information. We share data only with essential service providers (payment processors, scheduling tools) under contract that requires them to protect your data, and only when legally required by authorities.

5. Data Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on access-controlled systems. Access is limited to staff who need it to serve your project. While no system is 100% secure, we maintain industry-standard safeguards.

6. Data Retention

Quote and project records are retained for up to 7 years to honour warranties and meet tax obligations. Newsletter subscriptions remain until you unsubscribe (one click, instantly).

7. Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate details
  • Deletion — request erasure where we are not legally required to keep records
  • Object — opt out of marketing at any time

Email privacy@roypainter.com to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be announced at the top of this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
